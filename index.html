About

I'm a Development and Operations Engineer at CranioRehab, a small medical device company focused on craniofacial rehabilitation, where I design and build mechanical and electronic systems and work at the intersection of manufacturing, medical devices, and embedded systems. My interests include cancer research, medical device design, and product development, and I hold a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering with a minor in Electrical Engineering from the University of Colorado Boulder (2026). I'm always looking for new challenges and opportunities to grow as an engineer, recent projects are leading me more into the area of surgical monitors.
